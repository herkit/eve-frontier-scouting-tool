# Defintions

## Sites
Sites are explorable areas in a solar system. They have different content based
on classification and subclassification. 

Classifications
- Rift
- Landscape
- Asteroid
- Ruin
- Combat

## Scout report
A report of in game sites present in a solar system.

The report includes:
- Time when the report was created
- Name and internal (integer) ID of the system being explored
- The kind of site and the number of occurrences of those sites are present
